## **🤖 AI WORKFORCE MULTI-LANGUAGE CAPABILITIES**

### **1️⃣ Voice Communication**

AI workers will speak and understand all six languages:

**Inbound Calls**: Automatically detect the caller's language and respond appropriately.

**Outbound Calls**: Choose language based on carrier, driver, broker, or shipper profile.

**Example:**

**English**: "Hello, this is Trans Bot AI Dispatch, confirming Load #987 pickup at 3 PM."

**Hindi**: "नमस्ते, यह ट्रांस बॉट एआई डिस्पैच है, लोड #987 का पिकअप शाम 3 बजे है।"

**Punjabi**: "ਸਤ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ, ਇਹ ਟ੍ਰਾਂਸ ਬੋਟ ਏਆਈ ਡਿਸਪੈਚ ਹੈ, ਲੋਡ #987 ਦਾ ਪਿਕਅੱਪ 3 ਵਜੇ ਹੈ।"

### **2️⃣ Text Messaging & Chat**

**Multi-lingual SMS, email, and chat** built into the communication module.

**Dynamic translation powered by AI** — automatically translates messages into the recipient's preferred language.

**Example Workflow:**
Broker sends rate confirmation in English →
Driver receives in Hindi or Punjabi automatically →
Driver's reply translated back into English for broker visibility.

### **3️⃣ Negotiation & Scheduling**

**Rate Negotiation in Any Language:**
- AI negotiates rates with carriers or shippers in their native tongue.
- All final agreements synced and stored in English for auditing.

**Scheduling Appointments:**
- Warehouse and yard teams see schedules in local language for clarity.

### **4️⃣ Document Processing**

**OCR and document handling** now support multi-lingual text:

- Bills of Lading (BOL), Proof of Delivery (POD), customs forms.
- Hindi and Punjabi documents automatically scanned, classified, and translated into English for operations and compliance.

### **5️⃣ Real-Time Language Switching**

**Automatic detection of preferred language** using:

- Caller ID profiles
- Carrier and driver portal settings
- Shipper CRM data

AI dynamically switches languages mid-conversation if needed.
